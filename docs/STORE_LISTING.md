## Description

The universe ain't giving you signs. They're targeted ads, dear.

PrivacyBrew shows who sees your visit to each website, in simple words. It also blocks known trackers and ads.

What you get:
- A report card for each website: trackers, ads, outside scripts, cookies and fingerprinting.
- Tracker and ad blocking, on by default. Turn it off for one website if it breaks.
- Cookie pop-up handling that tries to reject optional cookies.
- A "do not sell my data" request (Global Privacy Control) to every website.

What PrivacyBrew does not do:
- It does not send your data anywhere. All checks run in your browser.
- It has no account, no analytics and no ads.
- It cannot block all video ads.

Tracker list: Disconnect (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Ad list: EasyList.
